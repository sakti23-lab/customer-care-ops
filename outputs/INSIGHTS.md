# Insights — Customer Care Operations

*Generated 2026-08-28 from a synthetic travel-themed ticket set (5,305 tickets, 2026-01-01 to 2026-07-01).*

## Headline KPIs
- **5,305 tickets** handled; **333** still open.
- Average resolution time **14.7 hrs**; first-contact resolution (<24h) **79.6%**.
- Average CSAT **3.80/5**, top-2-box **66.6%**.

## What the data says
1. **Volume driver:** `Booking Change` is the largest category (1,127 tickets) — and a prime candidate for self-service deflection.
2. **Experience gap:** `Complaint` shows the lowest CSAT (3.70) with avg resolution 24.3h — the clearest improvement target.
3. **Segment signal:** `First-time` travellers are the highest-volume segment; their CSAT (3.79) is the benchmark to protect.

## Recommended actions (data-driven)
- Automate `Booking Change` intake (App/WhatsApp) to lift first-contact resolution above 85%.
- Stand up an SLA on `Complaint` at <17h and re-measure CSAT after 30 days.
- Track these KPIs live in a Looker Studio / Tableau dashboard (see dashboard/looker_spec.md).
